# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads ensure product quality and reliability through testing strategies, test plans, and validation of acceptance criteria. They work with Product Managers to clarify expected outcomes and with Developers to identify defects and improve test coverage.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Execute and coordinate manual and automated testing
- Identify, triage, and track defects and regressions with Developers
- Collaborate on test automation and continuous integration
- Validate release readiness and communicate quality metrics to the team

### Goals
- Ensure the product meets quality standards and acceptance criteria
- Reduce defects reaching production
- Enable fast, confident releases through effective testing

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and definition-of-done discussions with Product Managers and Developers
- Standup updates on test progress, defects, and blockers
- Test results and release-readiness recommendations to Project Managers
- Defect reports and QA signoff for releases

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide architectural guidance, make and communicate technical design decisions, and assess technical risks. They guide and mentor Developers while helping Product Managers and Project Managers understand technical constraints and trade-offs.

### Responsibilities
- Design system architecture and technical approaches for features
- Review technical proposals and guide design decisions
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Mentor Developers and guide code quality practices
- Coordinate technical dependencies and integration planning
- Explain technical constraints and trade-offs to product and project leadership

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable, and reliable software
- Reduce technical debt and architectural risk
- Help the team deliver efficiently through clear technical guidance

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions with Developers
- Guidance and mentoring through code reviews
- Technical risk and dependency updates during planning and execution
- Trade-off discussions with Product Managers and Project Managers

---

## Business Analyst / Requirements Specialist

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge stakeholders and the delivery team by gathering requirements and translating business needs into clear user stories and acceptance criteria. They work with Product Managers to shape scope and with Developers to clarify intended behavior.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business requirements from stakeholders
- Analyze scope, dependencies, and business impact
- Collaborate with Product Managers on user stories and acceptance criteria
- Validate that proposed solutions address business objectives
- Document business processes and use cases
- Support User Acceptance Testing (UAT) coordination

### Goals
- Align business needs with technical delivery
- Minimize scope confusion through clear requirements
- Enable stakeholders to validate solutions against their intent

### Typical Communication
- Requirements-gathering workshops with stakeholders and Product Managers
- User-story refinement with Product Managers and Developers
- Requirement and scope updates to Project Managers
- UAT coordination and feedback with stakeholders and the delivery team

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, help set priorities, and provide resource commitment and approval for major decisions. They partner with Product Managers to define value and with Project Managers to stay aligned on delivery, risks, and decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and success criteria with Product Managers
- Prioritize and approve scope changes within their authority
- Help remove organizational barriers and resolve escalated blockers
- Communicate project status and business value to broader leadership
- Provide feedback and validate that solutions align with business intent
- Participate in release and deployment decisions as appropriate

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Maintain stakeholder alignment and support
- Enable timely decisions and escalation

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder updates and reviews with Project Managers
- Prioritization and business-value discussions with Product Managers
- Go/no-go decisions and approval checkpoints
- Risk, blocker, and release-readiness escalations

---

## Support / Customer Success Manager

### Role Summary
Support and Customer Success Managers represent the voice of the customer. They bring customer feedback and real-world usage insights to Product Managers and Developers, and coordinate with Project Managers to prepare customers and support teams for changes.

### Responsibilities
- Gather customer feedback and feature-related support issues
- Identify support training and documentation needs
- Contribute supportability considerations to acceptance criteria and the definition of done
- Share customer adoption metrics and usage patterns with Product Managers
- Coordinate customer communications for releases with Project Managers
- Identify usability gaps and potential customer impact

### Goals
- Improve customer adoption and satisfaction
- Reduce support burden through thoughtful product design and documentation
- Provide early warning of customer impact from product changes

### Typical Communication
- Feature kickoff and acceptance-criteria discussions with Product Managers and Developers
- Support-readiness and documentation reviews before releases
- Customer feedback sessions and analysis shared with the delivery team
- Release notes and customer communications coordinated with Project Managers
