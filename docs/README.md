# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

This collection describes how OctoAcme takes cross-functional work from an initial idea through delivery and improvement. The approach is customer-first and iterative, with clear ownership, data-informed decisions, and regular communication. Project Managers coordinate delivery, schedules, risks, and communications; Product Managers define outcomes and prioritize work; developers and QA validate the solution; and stakeholders provide input and approvals.

## Project lifecycle

1. **Initiation** — Validate the business need and measurable outcome, align stakeholders, identify risks and resources, and decide whether to proceed.
2. **Planning** — Turn approved work into a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria, estimates, dependencies, a Definition of Done, and a release and milestone plan.
3. **Execution & tracking** — Deliver in small increments, track progress on the project board, communicate regularly, manage blockers and risks, and test and review work.
4. **Release & deployment** — Confirm release criteria, deploy and verify the change, communicate the release, and use the rollback or incident plan if needed.
5. **Retrospective & continuous improvement** — Review what went well and what could improve, then assign and track a small number of actionable follow-ups.
